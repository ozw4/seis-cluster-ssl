# Same-survey pretraining claim boundary

Self-supervised pretraining on a complete survey can include unlabelled
amplitudes from regions used by a later within-survey evaluation. Results using
such an encoder support a same-survey, transductive representation-learning
claim.

They do not by themselves establish an inductive spatial holdout, transfer to
an unseen survey, absence of downstream label leakage, or improved task
performance. Those claims require separate controls and downstream evidence.
